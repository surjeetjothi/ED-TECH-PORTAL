Role Management
 Objective
A role represents a set of permissions assigned based on job responsibilities. For example, an
“Administrator” role might have permissions to manage user accounts and sensitive data, while
an “Employee” role may only access essential applications and documents
 Assumptions
Root_Super_Admin - This role is only visible to user if they are logged in as root_super_admin.
For all other user who have access to Role management Menu they should never be able to see
this Role.
